Dataset Name: Diamonds Dataset
Description: Contains prices and attributes of nearly 54,000 diamonds.

Features / Columns:
- price: Price in US dollars ($326 - $18,823) [Target Variable]
- carat: Weight of the diamond (0.2 - 5.01)
- cut: Quality of the cut (Fair, Good, Very Good, Premium, Ideal)
- color: Diamond color, ranging from D (best) to J (worst)
- clarity: Measurement of how clear the diamond is (I1, SI2, SI1, VS2, VS1, VVS2, VVS1, IF)
- depth: Total depth percentage = z / mean(x, y) = 2 * z / (x + y)
- table: Width of top of diamond relative to widest point
- x: Length in 3.95 mm
- y: Width in 3.98 mm
- z: Depth in 2.43 mm
